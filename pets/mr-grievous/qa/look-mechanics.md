The pet is a rigid mechanical mask on an articulated neck above a planted torso. Keep torso, arms, hips, legs, and feet fixed in position and scale. Turn the mask head about the neck; the central ivory ridge, paired red eyes, cheek vents, and side discs rotate together as one physical mask. The eyes remain embedded in the faceplate, never slide independently. For up/down pitch, tip the mask around the neck hinge and let the upper neck flex slightly. For left/right yaw, reveal the near side disc and occlude the far eye/cheek naturally. Crown fins follow the head rigidly. Avoid whole-body rotation or warping.
