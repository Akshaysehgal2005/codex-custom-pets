Sauron is a rigid black-iron helmet with a narrow embedded ember-red visor and tall crown spikes on a compact articulated body. Keep torso, feet, shoulders, and legs anchored in look rows. Pivot the entire helmet at the neck; the visor slit, central ridge, cheek plates, and spike crown move together as one rigid assembly. Let the near side disc grow visible while the far side occludes on yaw; tip the whole faceplate on pitch. Never slide the visor independently or turn/warp the whole body.
